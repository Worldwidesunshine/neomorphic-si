NEOMORPHIC™: The Analog Intelligence Infrastructure Layer

“Nature is Analog. Intelligence Should Be Too.”

Trinity Sky | October 2026

Beyond the Age of Inference

Section titled “Beyond the Age of Inference”

Frequency, Vibration, and the NEOMORPHIC™ Return to Analog Intelligence

The artificial intelligence industry is confronting hard physical constraints. Multi-gigawatt data-centre plans reveal the scale of today’s infrastructure ambitions.[1] The Age of Inference brings those constraints into everyday deployment, where systems must deliver useful responses and real-world decisions within limits of energy, memory, and time. When so much work goes into moving information and rebuilding context, the architecture itself deserves to be questioned.

The next era of AI may not come from scaling digital infrastructure indefinitely. It may come from fundamentally rethinking how intelligence is represented, processed, and transmitted.

Intelligence is useful when a system can reach what it knows and respond in time. That can require repeatedly finding information, moving it, and rebuilding the context needed to use it. NEOMORPHIC™ is built to eliminate that repeated work.

The scale of the opportunity is substantial. The International Energy Agency projects $3.9 trillion in total data-centre investment from 2026 to 2030 in its Base Case, including supporting energy infrastructure. Energy supply and memory availability already constrain the buildout.[1]

Trinity Sky has built NEOMORPHIC™, an analog intelligence infrastructure layer that runs on digital hardware. It represents information in virtual space, drawing on frequency, phase, and harmonic resonance to organize how information is remembered, processed, and shared.[2]

We have working technology. Our benchmarks prove memory recall accuracy of 99.9% and memory recall latency of 13.834 microseconds.[2] NEOMORPHIC™ makes more intelligence available within the resources a system already has.

If NEOMORPHIC™ can solve the memory bottleneck, what is that worth?

How information lives in virtual space

Section titled “How information lives in virtual space”

NEOMORPHIC™ represents information as distributed patterns in a virtual frequency space. This space is a mathematical structure held in digital memory. Ordinary digital processors carry out the operations within it.[2]

A memory is a pattern of relationships

Section titled “A memory is a pattern of relationships”

Frequency describes how often a wave repeats. Phase describes its position within a cycle. NEOMORPHIC™ represents these properties numerically. Each memory consists of many numerical components in frequency space. Their phase relationships form the memory pattern. Patterns can also be combined to encode an item together with its associations and context.

Resonance helps identify the memory

Section titled “Resonance helps identify the memory”

Recall begins with a cue, such as a known detail or part of a question. The cue is represented in the same space and used to recover a candidate memory. The system then measures how closely that recovered pattern agrees with a stored item.[2]

Resonance describes how compatible patterns align in this virtual space. That agreement is measured as coherence, helping the system identify the intended memory. Digital computation evaluates these relationships.

For example, a stored association might link a machine, its operating condition, and an observed vibration. A cue containing the machine and condition can help recover the related observation. The relationship is part of what the system remembers.

Information and its context are represented together,
so a useful cue can lead back to a stored memory.

Connecting analog inputs and outputs

Section titled “Connecting analog inputs and outputs”

Analog inputs, such as sound or vibration, are converted into digital representations through suitable interfaces. Information that is already digital can enter directly. After processing, results can remain digital or return through analog outputs. The same virtual representation supports memory, processing, and exchange.

Remembering and sharing what matters

Section titled “Remembering and sharing what matters”

Imagine an expert who must reopen an archive and assemble the relevant context before every new answer. The knowledge is there. The delay comes from making it useful again.

A computer can also hold the information it needs and spend time waiting for that information to arrive. When memory access and movement hold back the task, more processing power alone may not remove the delay.[3] This is the memory bottleneck.

By encoding information together with its associations, NEOMORPHIC™ gives recall a structure to work with. It is built to reduce the work required to recover and reuse what a system knows, including context that would otherwise need to be rebuilt.

An example of intelligence exchange

Section titled “An example of intelligence exchange”

Imagine two devices monitoring the same machine. One has already linked a change in vibration to the machine’s recent operating history. It could share the relevant finding and context so the second device can continue the task with less work spent interpreting that history again.

Transmitting intelligence means sharing organized representations that carry both information and the associations needed to use it. A compatible receiving system can use that context to continue the task.

Share the context another system needs to continue,
with less work to rebuild it at the destination.

These representations still travel through ordinary communication channels. NEOMORPHIC™ preserves their useful frequency and phase relationships as they move between compatible systems, so the receiving system can work with the encoded context.

Researchers have explored a related general idea: designing communication around what the receiving AI system needs to do.[4] The opportunity for NEOMORPHIC™ is to connect memory, processing, and exchange so that useful work can continue across devices.

Memory recall in microseconds

Section titled “Memory recall in microseconds”

A microsecond is one millionth of a second. The reported memory recall latency of 13.834 microseconds equals 13,834 nanoseconds, or 0.013834 milliseconds.

A 99.9% recall accuracy rate corresponds to about 999 correct recalls per 1,000 attempts. This describes recalling the intended stored item in the accuracy test.

Why these results matter

Section titled “Why these results matter”

Fast, accurate memory recall can help an application reach the information it needs sooner. That creates an opportunity to spend more of its resources on the task itself.

Recall latency measures the memory operation. A complete AI response also includes work such as understanding an input, processing information, and producing an output. The practical test is whether the application completes the same task, at the required quality, using less time, memory, communication, energy, or money.

What solving the memory bottleneck could unlock

Section titled “What solving the memory bottleneck could unlock”

When memory holds a system back, improving it can make the rest of the system more useful. That can change the cost of an existing service or make a new product possible.

Where it helpsWhat an improvement could enable
Existing hardwareMore completed tasks from the same equipment, lower cost per task, or less need for additional hardware.
Local devicesMore capability within a device’s available memory and power, with fewer tasks sent to a remote service.
Connected systemsLess repeated work to interpret information and rebuild context as tasks move between devices.

A simple example of customer value

Section titled “A simple example of customer value”

Suppose a group of deployments has $1 billion in relevant annual operating costs. A measured 15% net reduction would save $150 million each year. That calculation includes the cost of adopting and running the new layer.

$1 billion in annual operating costs × 15% net reduction

A customer could also use an efficiency gain to do more within the same budget. For example, a device might handle a task locally that previously required a larger machine or a remote service.

The infrastructure opportunity

Section titled “The infrastructure opportunity”

Because NEOMORPHIC™ runs on digital hardware, its commercial path can include software integration, licensing, and product partnerships. A repeatable advantage could become valuable across many products and deployments.

The size of that value depends on the benefit customers can measure, the cost of adoption, and how widely the technology is used. Customer savings, provider revenue, and company valuation are separate quantities.

The larger possibility is more intelligence from the resources already available, with less work spent finding, moving, and rebuilding what a system knows.
