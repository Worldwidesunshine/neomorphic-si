Executive One-Liners & Interactive Schematics

Section titled “Executive One-Liners & Interactive Schematics”
JLE. 01 · DATA MOVEMENT PHYSICS 20,000× NETWORK WIN

1. Where a Byte Travels Changes Its Energy Cost by 10,000,000×

Moving a byte across the internet costs nearly 10 million times more energy than moving it on-chip.

  • On-chip HBM2 memory moves data for just 3.97 picojoules per bit; internet transport costs 27 microjoules per bit.
  • A single 3,549-byte compressed catalog transfer across the internet saves 1.20 Joules — 20,000× more energy than the native decoder spends unpacking it.
  • Result: software decoding keeps 99.995% of the energy savings across every network and cloud transfer.
JLE. 02 · SILICON DECODER ARCHITECTURE 204B ADDRESSES / JOULE

2. 27 Simple Operations in Silicon — Zero Multipliers Required

A dedicated hardware decoder unpacks up to 204 billion exact addresses per joule.

  • Each address takes an average of just 27.05 basic bit operations (shifts, masks, and integer adds) with no multipliers.
  • In 7 nm silicon, decoding costs just 4.9 to 32.9 picojoules per address — retaining 57.5% to 99.6% of net energy savings directly on HBM2 and DDR3 memory paths.
  • At 50 GB/s memory bandwidth, one tiny decoder block streams 12.5 billion addresses per second on 0.025 to 0.375 Watts.
JLE. 03 · THE 211× ECONOMIC MULTIPLIER 211× VALUE PER JOULE

3. A Saved Joule of GPU Time Is Worth 211× Its Electricity Cost

An AI system doesn’t buy bytes or raw electricity — it buys decisions.

  • A median AI decision consumes 864 Joules (0.24 Wh), of which raw electricity is just 0.8% of the cost ($0.0000204).
  • The other 99.2% ($0.00250 per decision) is the cost of the AI accelerator time occupied while moving and waiting on memory.
  • Every joule saved frees high-value GPU capacity worth 211× the price of the electricity itself.
JLE. 04 · COMPOUNDING HOP × BYTE CUTS UP TO 96.1% ENERGY CUT

4. Fewer Bytes × Fewer Hops = Exponential Energy Savings

Shrinking address catalogs by 61% and cutting memory hops in half eliminates 80.5% of movement energy.

  • Same path, 61.0% fewer bytes: immediate 61.0% reduction in data-movement energy.
  • 50% fewer hops + 61.0% fewer bytes: 80.5% total reduction in movement energy.
  • 90% fewer hops + 61.0% fewer bytes: 96.1% total reduction in movement energy.
